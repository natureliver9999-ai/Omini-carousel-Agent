# Hook formulas

The cover headline is the whole negotiation. Six formulas, each with its failure mode.

## 1. Contrarian claim
> "Half of what you know about carousels is backwards."

Creates an identity threat that only reading resolves. **Fails** when the deck agrees with everyone. Refute a named belief by slide 3.

## 2. Specific number + outcome + timeframe
> "We cut render time from 40s to 0.4s with one contract."

Specificity is the cheapest credibility. **Fails** when the number is invented — the linter and the schema both refuse unsourced proof slides.

## 3. Mistake callout
> "Your carousel dies on slide two."

Names a cost the reader already paid. **Fails** if the fix arrives four slides later; land it by slide 3.

## 4. Before / after
> "Same audience, same offer, 3× the saves."

The delta is the story. **Fails** when "before" is a strawman. Use `contrast_split` with identical geometry so the flip is visible.

## 5. Question that indicts
> "If your best post is five slides long, why are you posting fifteen?"

Answerable only by swiping. **Fails** when the answer is obvious from the question.

## 6. Receipt
> "67.5 million posts later, carousels still lead engagement."

Borrowed authority with a trail. **Fails** when the source is a blog quoting a blog. Name the study, the sample, the date — on the slide.

---

## Anti-patterns (auto-flagged)

| Pattern | Example | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Throat-clearing | "In today's fast-paced world…" | Burns the one frame everyone sees |
| Self-introduction | "Hi, I'm Alex and I help…" | Nobody has agreed to care |
| Vague promise | "Unlock the power of…" | No checkable outcome |
| Adjective stack | "cutting-edge, seamless, game-changing" | Unfalsifiable |
| Topic label | "Social media strategy tips" | Nothing to resolve, nothing to swipe for |

## Pre-publish score

Answer yes to at least four:

1. ≤ 12 words?
2. Names a cost, a number, or a belief?
3. Someone could disagree with it?
4. Works with no caption and no context?
5. Creates a question the next slide answers?

## Footer micro-hooks (≤ 7 words)

- "Slide 4: the metric nobody checks"
- "Next: the part that breaks"
- "The fix is one rule"
- "Here's where it goes wrong"

Never: "Swipe for more", "Stay tuned", "You won't believe".
