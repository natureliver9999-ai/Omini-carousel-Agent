Why Your Carousel Dies On Slide Two

Omni Carousel · instagram · saves · Editorial Print
Slides6
Canvas1080 × 1350
Themeeditorial

Caption

If your carousel gets impressions but not saves, the problem is almost never the hook.

It is slide two. Instagram will happily re-serve your post starting at frame two, and if that frame introduces a topic instead of raising a stake, the reader is gone.

Diagnostic:
1. Does frame two make sense alone?
2. Does it raise a question or answer one?
3. Is there a reason to keep swiping?

Three yes answers, or rewrite the frame.
