## Caption

An agent does not "design" a carousel. It fills a contract, and a deterministic engine renders it.

That split is the whole trick:

· The model decides what to say
· The playbook decides the structure
· The spec is the only contract
· The engine decides every pixel
· The bundle carries the provenance
